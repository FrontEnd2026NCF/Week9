About the index

The Case-Shiller index tracks home prices over time. It is an index, not a dollar price. The value was set to about 100 in January 2000.

An index value of 200 means prices are about double the January 2000 level.

What you can learn from it

  • How much prices rose or fell between two dates.
  • Whether a city grew faster or slower than the national average.
  • When prices peaked and when they bottomed out.
